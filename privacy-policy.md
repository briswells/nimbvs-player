# Nimbvs Player — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 20, 2026**

## Overview

Nimbvs Player is an audiobook client that connects to your self-hosted Audiobookshelf server. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## What the app accesses

- **Your Audiobookshelf server**: The app connects directly to server(s) you configure. Your server URL, username, and authentication token are stored locally on your device in the iOS Keychain. They are never sent to us or any third party.
- **Listening progress**: Playback progress is stored locally on your device and synced directly to your Audiobookshelf server. No data passes through our servers.
- **Downloaded files**: Audiobook files you download are stored on your device only.

## What we do NOT collect

- No analytics or telemetry
- No crash reporting to third parties
- No advertising or tracking
- No personal information of any kind

## Data storage

All data (server credentials, book metadata, listening progress, downloads) is stored locally on your device. The app communicates only with the Audiobookshelf server(s) you configure — there is no backend service operated by us.

## Third-party services

Nimbvs Player uses no third-party SDKs, analytics, or services.
